A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Can I help as an individual?+

Yes. We welcome conversations with individuals as well as companies, community groups, and other organizations. You don’t need a large contribution to start a conversation.

What should I include when offering a space?+

Tell us the location, approximate capacity, availability, and facilities, such as internet access and power outlets. We can then discuss whether it could work for a cohort session.

Can I offer used laptops?+

Yes. Please share the number of devices, their make and model, specifications if available, condition, whether chargers are included, and where they’re located. We’ll discuss suitability and the practical arrangements with you first.

Do you accept support from outside Ireland?+

We’re a registered nonprofit company in Ireland and welcome learners from different parts of the world. Get in touch wherever you’re based so we can discuss how your offer could help, including any location or delivery considerations.

What if I can help in another way?+

We’d like to hear your idea. Email info@platformexplorers.com to start a conversation, or get in touch about mentoring if you’d like to share your time and experience.
