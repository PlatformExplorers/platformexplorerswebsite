Who we’re here for

Put your knowledge to work.

You might be looking for your first role in tech, changing specialisms, or working out what comes next after a few years in the industry. Our cohorts give you a place to apply what you know, with a mentor and a team to work alongside.

Platform Explorers welcomes people from anywhere in the world. You’ll work on projects, discuss your decisions, and practise explaining your contribution—the same skills you’ll need when working with colleagues or speaking to a recruiter.

Start with the problem. Before choosing a platform or tool, learn to understand the challenge, consider your options, and explain why your solution fits.

How the cohort works

Individual work, team projects, and guidance from a mentor.

Individual projects

Build your own solution.
With a mentor alongside you.

Start with a problem, consider your options, and put your knowledge to work. Your mentor offers guidance and feedback as you develop your own project.

Mentored team projects

Bring your skills
to a shared project.

Join a team and work with a mentor towards a solution you can deliver together. Divide tasks, take responsibility for your contribution, and learn from how others approach the work.

Team discussions

Learn to explain
your thinking.

Team calls are a place to share progress, discuss decisions, and work through challenges. Practise explaining your approach to people with different backgrounds and listening to their ideas.

In the team discussion

  • What problem are we solving?
  • Why did we choose this approach?
  • What do we need to work through?
Make space for questions.

Support with your job search

Throughout the cohort, we help you prepare to talk about your work and the experience you’re gaining. Explore the support below.

Mock interviews

Practise talking about your work.+

Work through practice interviews and get feedback on how you explain your projects. Talk through the problem you solved, the choices you made, and your contribution, so you can describe your experience clearly to a recruiter or hiring team.

CV reviews

Show the experience you’re building.+

Get feedback on your CV and guidance on how to present your skills and project experience. Make your contribution clear, including what you built independently and what you delivered with your team.

Industry conversations

Hear from recruiters and tech professionals.+

Meet professionals who share their career journeys and recruiters who explain what they look for. Ask questions, learn from their experience, and take those insights into your job search.

A community beyond the cohort

Stay connected after the cohort through our Discord community.

We share job opportunities and resources, ask questions, and help each other work through challenges. You’ll meet people at different stages of their careers and professionals who share what they’ve learned along the way.

Common questions

Do you have a Discord community?+

Yes. Our Discord community is a place to connect with fellow explorers, find shared opportunities and resources, ask questions, and share what you’re learning. Join our Discord community to get involved.

Is Platform Explorers right for me?+

We welcome people starting their careers, changing paths, or already working in tech and looking for direction. If you have foundational knowledge and want to build practical experience, collaborate with others, and communicate your work more confidently, this community could be for you.

What knowledge should I bring?+

You should have foundational knowledge in the area you want to explore. The cohort helps you apply that knowledge through individual and team projects. Both people building their first project experience and those developing an existing career can benefit.

When does the next cohort start?+

Follow our LinkedIn page for announcements about the next cohort, available tracks, and how to apply. We share confirmed details there.

Can I contribute as a mentor or partner?+

We welcome conversations with industry professionals and organizations interested in supporting early-career talent. Email info@platformexplorers.com to introduce yourself and how you’d like to contribute.
