01 / WHERE IT STARTED

The gap was familiar.
So we set out to bridge it.

Knowing the fundamentals doesn’t always make the next career step clear. You may be starting out, changing direction, or already working in tech. You still need opportunities to apply your knowledge, make decisions, collaborate, and explain what you can do.

Rachel Irabor and Juan Ojochemi Idowu founded Platform Explorers to help people take that next step. Participants begin with individual project work, then build in teams with mentors. Along the way, they practise analysing problems, choosing suitable tools, sharing responsibilities, and presenting their solutions.

A place to ask questions. To try, rethink, and try again.
And to build confidence through the work itself.

02 / WHAT WE’RE WORKING TOWARDS

OUR MISSION

Help turn knowledge
into experience.

Help people at different stages of a tech career apply what they know through individual projects, mentored teamwork, and problem-solving. Develop the communication and professional skills to explain their work and take their next step.

OUR VISION

Confidence to take
the next career step.

We want people to approach new opportunities with practical experience, a clearer sense of direction, and confidence in what they can contribute. Our community brings together learners and professionals from different parts of the world.

03 / HOW WE SHOW UP

Practical by design.
Human at heart.

The tools matter.
So does how we work together.

01 / LEARN BY DOING

Make room for practice.

Begin by understanding the business problem. Explore the options, choose a suitable platform or tool, and explain why your approach addresses the challenge.

02 / GROW TOGETHER

Share what you know.

Build independently, then work in a mentored team. Divide tasks, discuss progress, learn from feedback, and present the work you’ve delivered together.

03 / BUILD CONFIDENCE

Support the whole journey.

CV reviews, mock interviews, industry speakers, and recruiter insights help participants prepare for opportunities and explain their project experience with confidence.
